# The Arabic glossary

Arabic mode (`js/i18n.js`, `js/i18n/ar.js`) keeps to one word for one idea: the
terms of the Gulf school curricula, said the way a physics teacher says them to
an A-level student. Add to this list before using a new term in `ar.js`.

Symbols, numbers and units stay as on an A-level paper: *u, v, s, t, a, g, θ*,
digits 0–9, m s⁻¹. The brand stays in Latin: SUVAT, Suvat.
