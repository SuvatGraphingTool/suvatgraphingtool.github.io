# The past-paper catalogue

Every A-level SUVAT question the library loop has looked at: where it was set,
what it is about in our own words, what it needs from the app, and what became
of it. **No question text is stored here or anywhere in the repo.** Exam
questions are copyrighted, so an entry is a reference, a link to the paper, a
paraphrase written for this file, and the numbers the paper gives.

What students see is the shelf on screen one, built from
[`js/library/questions.js`](../../js/library/questions.js). This file is the
working record behind it, including the questions that never reach the shelf.

## Status

| status | meaning |
|---|---|
| **built** | a library scenario was made for it. The section below says what it is and what is general about it. |
| **shelved** | an existing scenario answers it, and it is on the shelf with its numbers. |
| **covered** | an existing scenario answers it, but it is not on the shelf yet. |
| **next** | it needs something the app cannot show yet, and it is queued. |
| **out** | it needs a topic outside constant-acceleration SUVAT. The reason is given. |

Scope is anything constant-acceleration SUVAT can solve: projectiles, vertical
motion, straight-line motion, vectors in **i** and **j**, travel graphs, and
two objects meeting or overtaking. Forces and F = ma, friction, connected
particles, momentum, variable acceleration and energy are out.
